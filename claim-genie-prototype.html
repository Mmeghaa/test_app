Health Insurance Claims Intimation

Claim Genie integrated with Claim Lens

A Figma-like, clickable HTML prototype for reimbursement hospitalization claims. It covers member selection, reimbursement vs. cashless routing, applicable-benefit multi-selection, claim-system matching, document upload, Claim Lens AI validation, digital form quality check, hidden bank validation, consent, and reference generation.

Designed process flow

1
Login outcomeSelect insured member, then choose Reimbursement or Cashless.
2
Claim intentReimbursement shows File a new Claim and Track your existing Claim; Cashless keeps the current flow.
3
Applicable benefits onlyClickable pictorial cards for hospitalization, before hospitalization, and after hospitalization with multi-select support.
4
Claim Lens validationDocuments are checked for mandatory availability, extraction, surgical-report requirement, and fallback manual form entry.
5
Digital QC and submitAuto-filled form, edit controls, masked contacts, background penny-drop validation, consent, and generated claim reference.
